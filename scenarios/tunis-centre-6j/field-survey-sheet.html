Tunis centre-ville J1–J6: lane and crossing field survey

  • Count vehicle lanes at the stop line, left to right as seen by an approaching driver. Do not count parking bays. Write each lane's arrows (e.g. L|T|TR); write ? where unmarked.
  • Bus-only or other restricted lanes go in their own column, not in the general count. If the count changes before the junction, tick "count changes" and note where: the way must be split before the value can be applied.
  • "Forward/backward" follow the OSM way's node order, not compass direction. The direction is printed on each row.
  • Every row needs a dated, geotagged photo showing the markings, plus the camera heading. Undated imagery or road width alone does not verify a count.
  • Speeds: Tunisia's urban limit is 50 km/h unless a sign shows a 30 or 70 km/h decision. Record the sign actually posted.
