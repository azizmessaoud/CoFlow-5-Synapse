# 10p seeded pedestrian-demand gate

The J0 controlled fixture uses seed 11 to produce five reproducible pedestrian
arrivals and complete crossing occupancy intervals. The public A3 seam exposes
typed active-crossing state with positive remaining clearance. Wait evidence is
reported as mean, P95, and maximum values, with zero clearance truncations.

This is controlled simulation evidence, not a roadside measurement or a
deployment claim.
