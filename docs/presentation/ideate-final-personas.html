Refused: a scraped map treated as truth, a camera that guesses ability, a chatbot that writes the light, a lives-saved claim. A later file we still owe — a measured crossing, a dated GTFS extract, a driver message, a corridor file — does not become a solution on this slide.

8 · Data analysis

Patterns we can name. Not a hotspot model.

  • From the press briefing of ONSR: inattention about 42% of crashes, speeding about 14% of crashes and about 30% of deaths, unsafe pedestrian crossing about 8% of crashes. These are cause classes in that briefing, not a model of our junction.
  • Men are the large majority of recorded deaths in the WHO profile and the investment case.
  • From our one matched seed: actuated finished more trips and had a lower mean wait. Max-Pressure did not win. Unfinished trips stayed in the count. That is David’s pattern.
  • From the emission-proxy fixture: one link improved and one worsened. That is displacement, not measured air. The school street is a place, not a person.

A risk factor for a specific intersection, hour, and weather type was not found.

9 · Heatmap

No hotspot map until the table exists.

A shaded map needs rows of intersection, time, and the thing being counted, plus the denominator. That table is not in the repository. Weather is not joined to a junction and an hour, so rain is not a layer on this map.

Will not draw: rain over roads, crash pins from the news, or a green map that hides the link whose proxy got worse.

Later, with the method printed on it: counts of a class, or delay, by link and by time window, with the sample size on the cell. A dark cell is not a cause and not a life saved.

Until that table exists, this stage stays a refusal. It is not an empty picture pretending to be a result.

10 · Accident prevention

How each solution addresses that person’s problem.

Who is affectedWhat changes for them
AmaraConflicting traffic stays red while the crossing is occupied. The fixture counted zero truncations. That is not a count of crashes prevented.
DavidA jammed scenario cannot look safe just because the average improved. Unfinished trips stay visible.
ChidiAn early bus does not steal the crossing from the person still in it.
RosaThe reason is kept, so a bad pattern can be found after the shift. She still does not change the light.
MarcusHe is not sent into a blocked exit, and Amara’s crossing is not cut. Civilian delay is logged beside his time. Not lives saved.
OmarA stale sensor is not called a healthy road, so a false all-clear is not the alert.

Fewer conflicts in the model. Not fewer deaths in Tunisia. No lives saved. No measured air. No Tunis deployment. No winner.
