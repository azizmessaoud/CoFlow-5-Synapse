## Question

What belongs under Evidence versus a Claim flag?

v2 already has a flags list of statements the Empathize pack must not make, plus cited primary sources under each User Persona. Draw the bar so Evidence stays cited primary-source claims, and forbidden statements stay Claim flags rather than quietly entering Evidence or Possible Solution.

## Answer

**Evidence** = cited primary-source claims that motivate a need:

1. **Tunisia practice** — law, agencies, open data, operator publications (TRANSTU, Décret 2000-149, MEHAT, ANPE, CODATU–AFD, official GTFS).
2. **Transfer literature** — DfT TAG, FHWA ATSPM/ASCT/TIM, MUTCD/HCM, NZTA/TfL wait, SCOOT/SCATS/Surtrac *product patterns*, peer-reviewed mechanism papers.

**Claim flags / Not Evidence:**

- IMATM Table 9 ▲ digits and interview/ride-along quotes (source self-labels placeholders).
- Invented survey % (AI distrust, rider abandon), wait 1.5×, unverified MUTCD 2023 0.8 m/s, flashover 3–5 min, EY as hard Evidence.
- SUMO/HBEFA as measured AQ or lives saved; scheduled GTFS as live AVL; Tunis “runs SCATS” because Rosa/Yuki exist.
- SMART numeric targets (mean ≤20 s, P95 −5%, etc.) — **proposed tests**, not literature thresholds.

**Insight bar:** mechanism statements I-1…I-10 may structure Empathize; each must carry Tunis and/or transfer Evidence in the alignment matrix, with ▲ digits listed under Not Evidence.
